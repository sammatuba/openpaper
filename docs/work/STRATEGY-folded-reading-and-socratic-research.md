# Folded reading and Socratic research in OpenPaper

Strategy recommendation for owner review, 2026-11-24. Source baseline `feat/spatial-workspace` at `daec9f8f`.

## Decision

Start with one saved finding. From the finding or its folded preview, **Open paper passage** reveals the evidence and **Test my understanding** asks one private question about it. Returning restores the same finding. Keep the owner-requested map as the project entry, all five reading levels, and the full project study path on Discuss. A researcher checking one surprising result should be able to answer one question and leave.

For example, suppose a paper reports a gain on one benchmark. The researcher opens the cited passage and chooses to be tested. The tutor asks, “Which comparison supports the reported gain?” After an attempt or a requested hint, feedback identifies the relevant lines and what the comparison leaves open. If the researcher wants to revise the shared interpretation, they edit and save the finding through Evidence. Their practice answer and skill estimate stay private from other members.

## Principles for AI-assisted research

- **Inspectable evidence.** Keep original wording, the authors’ interpretation, the researcher’s interpretation and generated feedback distinguishable. Expose the PDF and withhold source-dependent grading when the extraction cannot support it. The TARA field run found joined words and interleaved columns in real papers. A matching hash preserves extraction errors too. [TARA field report](REPORT-tara-e2e.md) · [NIST Generative AI Profile](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf)
- **Accountable synthesis.** A researcher explicitly reviews and adopts any proposed shared change. If AI-assisted text enters the research record, retain source version, purpose, model/version when known, date and the review decision. Show whether a conclusion covers a passage, paper or project collection. Formal reviews can then report what AI contributed and how it was checked. [Cochrane joint position](https://www.cochrane.org/about-us/news/setting-standards-responsible-ai-use-evidence-synthesis) · [Cochrane Handbook, reporting the review](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-iii)
- **Learning through retrieval and explanation.** Ask one question, allow an attempt or hint, then ground feedback in the passage. Accept a justified “the passage does not tell us.” In a longer session, revisit the idea using a different example. Retrieval and explanatory questioning have empirical support. This tutor’s grading accuracy still needs measurement. [IES practice guide, recommendations 5b and 7](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/PracticeGuide/1/Published)

## Sequence and evidence that would change the decision

1. **Prove the reading handoff with zero model calls.** Carry a stable finding reference from Evidence into its folded unit, open the original passage, and restore the same finding on return. Exercise a clean single-column source, a garbled two-column source, a changed source and lost access. If readers cannot locate or interpret the original, prioritize extraction and anchoring.
2. **Prove one private passage check.** Send the finding reference and expected source version to the server. Resolve both under current project access. Exercise interruption, retry after commit, missing progress and another member opening the conversation. Reuse message traces initially. Conflicting simultaneous turns that the trace cannot reconcile would justify versioned session writes.
3. **Evaluate before expanding.** Compare the current Brief/Discuss route with the proposed handoff. Observe whether first-time users can locate a result, distinguish it from its interpretation, answer one challenge and return without coaching. On a separately authorized real-model run, score question count per turn, correct supporting lines, warranted abstention and skill advances after independent attempts. Include a misleading summary, contradictory findings and an unanswerable question. Treat a private-state disclosure, grading from inaccessible evidence or an automatic shared edit as a failed check.

The first slice assumes comprehension at finding level is the immediate need. If users consistently want to study the entire paper, move the next entry to the paper reader. If they want to challenge relationships between findings, trial a two-source comparison with explicit support and counterevidence. The owner’s map-first design and the existing project course remain available throughout.
